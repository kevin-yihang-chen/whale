\section{Shared Scoring}
Answer extraction is host-owned. Invalid ground truth is a data error; an
incorrect or malformed model answer is an unsuccessful trajectory. Pair scores
require complete identity-matched predictions on both sides. No invalid or
missing sample is silently replaced with a zero or removed from the denominator.
\section{Reproducibility}
All seeds and condition names are preregistered. The same source family cannot
cross data roles. Every real run retains its configuration, source snapshot,
model and processor identities, decoder settings, raw responses, and terminal
Slurm allocation record. Shared-prefix computations are identified explicitly.
The Adam-reset restoration caveat is shared by every trained branch.
The public source model is Qwen3.5-4B revision
\href{https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B/tree/851bf6e806efd8d0a36b00ddf55e13ccb7b8cd0a}{851bf6e}.
All conditions start from the same audited BF16 initialization. Native RSFT
stores trainable parameters in FP32 and exports BF16 serving weights. Source,
derived initialization, native checkpoint and serving identities are recorded
separately, with the serving export checked against the native BF16 cast.
\section{Counterfactual Safety Audit}
Let $M_C$ be marginal competence and $F_C$ the fraction of pairs with exactly
one correct side. Then $P_C=M_C-F_C/2$ exactly. VETO-v3 uses jointly resampled
source-paired deltas against the incoming harness. Three candidate slots, two
metrics and both tails share the frozen nominal error allocation. Candidates
are \textbf{PASS}, \textbf{FAIL}, or \textbf{UNCERTAIN}; the latter two are excluded
and the incoming harness remains eligible. This empirical percentile-bootstrap
rule has no finite-population or post-training guarantee.

The earlier C64 point gate and paired-ranking V2 are retained as historical
diagnostics. V2 chose h1 over h3 by one C64 pair, but h1 was lower on the
independent R512 diagnostic and its paired interval included zero. R512 is thus
method-development information and cannot validate V3. Sequential repeated
looks are excluded until a valid repeated-look error allocation is frozen.
